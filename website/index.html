More features

Screen Share Privacy

While your screen is shared or recorded, the menu bar shows only the Runway mark. Your numbers come back when it ends.

Pace Ticks

A tick on every meter marks where usage should be right now. Run ahead of it and the bar turns yellow, then red, before you hit the limit.

Local Spend

Runway prices the session logs already on your disk, per model, and totals them for today, yesterday, and the last 30 days.

No Setup

It reads the same keychain items and auth files your CLIs already use. Install it, and every provider you are signed into shows up.

No Analytics

No telemetry, no accounts, no middleman server. Requests go from your Mac to each provider, and sync stays in your private iCloud. The code is MIT-licensed.

Native Swift

Launches in 0.3 seconds, opens the panel in a frame, and sits around 240 MB of memory. It refreshes in the background every five minutes.
